## What I do

I'm a Software Engineer at **Honeybee IoT Limited**, where I'm a core developer on a multi-module ERP platform serving enterprise manufacturing and retail clients — including **Bengal Group** and **Super Star Group**.

I own the **HRM, Sales, and Project Management** modules across web and mobile: Laravel and Vue.js on the frontend of that work, REST APIs powering the mobile apps, and third-party integrations (payment gateways, DATEV accounting, SMS/email) behind it. I've trained **150+ users** hands-on and supported **500+** in production.

Lately most of my energy goes into **shipping AI into products people actually use** — document extraction, natural-language data entry, and automated onboarding.

### 📱 Khorchapati — AI-Powered Family Finance App
**Live on Google Play** · Bilingual (বাংলা / English) · v1.5.0+

![Flutter](https://img.shields.io/badge/Flutter-02569B?style=flat-square&logo=flutter&logoColor=white)
![Dart](https://img.shields.io/badge/Dart-0175C2?style=flat-square&logo=dart&logoColor=white)
![Riverpod](https://img.shields.io/badge/Riverpod-4B32C3?style=flat-square)
![Firebase](https://img.shields.io/badge/Firebase-FFCA28?style=flat-square&logo=firebase&logoColor=black)
![SQLite](https://img.shields.io/badge/Drift%20(SQLite)-003B57?style=flat-square&logo=sqlite&logoColor=white)
![Socket.IO](https://img.shields.io/badge/Socket.IO-010101?style=flat-square&logo=socketdotio&logoColor=white)

- **AI bank-statement import** — a custom extraction API parses encrypted PDF statements from major banks, pulling 20+ categorized transactions with one-tap bulk review. Cuts entry time from ~30s to ~2s per transaction.
- **Voice transaction entry** — speech-to-text with NLP parsing that extracts amount, category, and notes from natural Bengali or English speech.
- **Fuzzy category matching** — auto-maps AI-suggested categories onto each user's own taxonomy, creating new ones on the fly when nothing matches.
- Multi-wallet architecture (6 types) on a shared Riverpod provider tree, with paginated history, cache invalidation across linked screens, and a feature-flag system for staged rollouts.

### 🏪 Swift POS — Multi-Tenant SaaS Point-of-Sale
**Per-tenant database isolation** · Launch a store and process a first sale in under 10 minutes

![Laravel](https://img.shields.io/badge/Laravel-FF2D20?style=flat-square&logo=laravel&logoColor=white)
![PHP](https://img.shields.io/badge/PHP-777BB4?style=flat-square&logo=php&logoColor=white)
![MySQL](https://img.shields.io/badge/MySQL-4479A1?style=flat-square&logo=mysql&logoColor=white)
![Stripe](https://img.shields.io/badge/Stripe-008CDD?style=flat-square&logo=stripe&logoColor=white)
![OpenAI](https://img.shields.io/badge/OpenAI%20API-412991?style=flat-square&logo=openai&logoColor=white)

- **AI onboarding engine** — ingests CSV/Excel catalogs, parses them through the OpenAI API, and auto-generates categories and products. Removes the single biggest setup barrier for non-technical shop owners.
- Full billing engine: cart management, split and multi-method payments, returns, refunds, voids, barcode support.
- End-to-end business modules — inventory with low-stock alerts, customer/supplier credit ledgers, purchase lifecycle, VAT, general ledger, exportable reports.
- Stripe via Laravel Cashier for subscriptions, trials, and scheduled plan changes, plus a Super Admin dashboard tracking MRR, ARR, ARPU, churn, and trial conversion.

### 💼 CareerConnect — Role-Based Job Portal
**Local LLM integration** · No external API dependency

![Laravel](https://img.shields.io/badge/Laravel-FF2D20?style=flat-square&logo=laravel&logoColor=white)
![Vue.js](https://img.shields.io/badge/Vue.js-4FC08D?style=flat-square&logo=vuedotjs&logoColor=white)
![MySQL](https://img.shields.io/badge/MySQL-4479A1?style=flat-square&logo=mysql&logoColor=white)
![Ollama](https://img.shields.io/badge/Ollama-000000?style=flat-square&logo=ollama&logoColor=white)

- **AI-assisted job drafting** through a local Ollama LLM — generates structured job descriptions from natural-language prompts, entirely self-hosted.
- Three isolated role dashboards (Admin, Employer, Applicant) with distinct permission sets.
- Token-auth REST APIs via Laravel Sanctum, with route protection, request validation, and role middleware.
- Relational schema and backend logic for jobs, applications, companies, skills, categories, and status histories.
